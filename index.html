Dal Rice

Recipe:

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan
  2. Add cumin seeds
  3. Toss in 2–3 red chillies or flakes
  4. Add curry leaves
  5. Add 2–3 garlic cloves, slightly crushed
  6. Mix in some diced onions
  7. Throw in 2–3 green chillies or flakes
  8. Season with salt
  9. Add turmeric powder (a.k.a. the golden magic)
  10. Add Mysore dal
  11. Pour some water and let it simmer
  12. Serve hot and enjoy!
